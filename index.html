Who we are

We're a group of friends building games on nights and weekends. We hope you'll connect with them and enjoy them as much as we enjoy making them.

Nathan

CO-FOUNDER · PRODUCER · 3D ARTIST A note from Nathan

Habitual daydreamer and optimist with a cybersecurity day job. I follow my interests wherever they lead (aka easily distracted), and magic still feels real to me. My favorite challenge is figuring out how we connect with people through the game, and I hope we're able to connect with you too!

Favorite games: Hades II, Golden Sun, Elden Ring, Overwatch (1), Zelda TotK, Smash Brothers.

Adam

CO-FOUNDER · CREATIVE DIRECTOR · PROGRAMMER · ARTIST A note from Adam

I am an old dad gamer that touches grass and would cease to exist without daily 400mg of caffeine. I love creating worlds and hope to some day build one you will enjoy, dear reader.

Favorite games of all time in no order: Terraria, Stardew Valley, Outward, Super Metroid, Elden Ring, Fire Emblem: Three Houses.

Karl

LEAD TECHNICAL DEVELOPER A note from Karl

I love a good story, a good puzzle, a good challenge, and a good fright, whether I'm experiencing them for myself or producing them for others.

Favorite games: Amnesia, Kingdom Hearts, The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask, Damned, Terraria, Black Myth: Wukong

Tiffany

ARTIST · SOCIALS A note from Tiffany

Creature Enthusiast, chronic daydreamer and probably the coolest nerd at any given social gathering.

Favorite Games: Subnautica, Skyrim, Baldur's Gate 3, Stardew Valley, and Medieval Dynasty.

Sadie

ARTIST A note from Sadie

I'm an artist who loves anime, video games, and dragons! I love experimenting with methods of drawing and doodling cute characters. Fun fact: when I was 12 I drew a unique dragon on every homework assignment and test I was handed. My teacher was cool about it, and made sure I got them back so I could add to my collection.

Favorite games: Hollow Knight, Majora's Mask, Wind Waker, Pokémon Leaf Green, The Binding of Isaac, Undertale
